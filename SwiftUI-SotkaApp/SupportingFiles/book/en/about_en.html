ABOUT THE AUTHORS


SOTKA is NOT another fitness program or training marathon. It is a free educational system aimed at transforming life through teaching the basics of physical activity and a healthy lifestyle.

Some people think we invented a conveyor belt, because everyone who reaches the end gets the same result. A good-looking body, excellent fitness, and a wave of compliments from people around them! Guys start getting genuine interest from girls. Girls start getting sincere attention from interesting guys. Our participants start seeing only the best around them. They change their lifestyle and their thinking.

But we invented nothing. All knowledge about an active life and proper nutrition has been known for many hundreds of years. We collected information from more than 1000 sources, from school textbooks in biology, chemistry and physics to the newest publications in foreign scientific journals. We processed it, structured it and presented it as a 100-day educational course. All you need to do is apply this knowledge. Without smart faces. Without trying to “catch out” the authors. Without showing off.

And it works again and again. This is more than just training. It is a lifestyle, a way of thinking, a real philosophy. That is why every year there are more of us. Every new success motivates dozens of new participants. That is why you are here now!
