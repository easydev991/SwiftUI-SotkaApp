<==== Вернуться к оглавлению


Today we talk about the goals we set for ourselves and for the participants of our program. We picked out 5 such goals:

1. Get basic knowledge about physical activity and a healthy lifestyle
2. Build a training habit
3. Raise your fitness level
4. Change your appearance
5. Find new friends

Goal #1. Get basic knowledge about physical activity and a healthy lifestyle




This goal is the most important one in our program.

In fact, the SOTKA program is first of all not a training program, but an educational one! Unlike others, we will not give ready-made answers and recommendations. Instead, we will tell and explain how, what and why. We want participants to understand what they are doing and why. That applies to doing the exercises, to nutrition, to the overall philosophy of Street Workout, and to many other questions.

So every day we will give you a certain amount of structured basic theoretical information on different topics!

Goal #2. Build a training habit




Training every day is easier than training from time to time. That is why the hardest thing for most people is to take the first step and start. After that they simply “enter” the mode and start seeing training as part of a normal day.

SOTKA is aimed first of all at beginners, so we built a program of daily sessions based on strength exercises with your own bodyweight, which you can do anywhere and anytime. Our motto is maximum availability of training! At the same time the structure of the program lets you pick the load individually so the body can recover in 24 hours. We simply take away all possible excuses to skip a session! Plus this approach removes dependence on a gym, bad weather and other outside conditions.

But in reality 100 days is only the beginning. We are sure that those who finish the program will want to keep moving and developing. Someone will decide to keep training outdoors, someone will choose iron, someone will start the sport they always dreamed of but did not have the preparation or the courage to sign up for. Physical development is an infinitely many-sided path that lasts a lifetime!

Goal #3. Raise your fitness level




The next goal by importance is to improve general physical preparation (GPP) and raise the numbers in the 3 main exercises — push-ups, pull-ups and squats. Unlike the previous one, this goal is more concrete and can be expressed in numbers.

Respectable numbers for men in bodyweight exercises (in one set):
- pull-ups - 25-30 reps
- push-ups - 80-100 reps
- two-leg squats - 150-200 reps


Respectable numbers for women in bodyweight exercises (in one set):
- pull-ups - 10-15 reps
- push-ups - 30-40 reps
- two-leg squats - 100-150 reps


Why not 200 push-ups and 500 squats? You can set those goals too, but why? After you reach the numbers above you can make your training (and your options) much more varied by adding new exercises:

- pull-ups -> muscle-ups
- push-ups -> dips
- two-leg squats -> jump squats

That is in general. Inside our program you can aim for the following results, based on statistics from participants of previous launches:

Pull-ups:
If at the start a participant does up to 10 reps, during the program that number will grow 2 (!!!) times.
If at the start a participant does from 10 to 15 reps, during the program that number will grow 1.5 (!) times.


Push-ups:
If at the start a participant does up to 25 push-ups, during the program that number will grow 2 (!!!) times.
If at the start a participant does from 25 to 50 push-ups, during the program that number will grow 1.5 (!) times.


Squats:
If at the start a participant does up to 30 reps, during the program that number will grow 3 (!!!) times.
If at the start a participant does from 30 to 100 reps, during the program that number will grow 1.5 (!) times.


!!! IMPORTANT!!! We do NOT GUARANTEE these results, but we DO GUARANTEE that such results are realistic. Thousands of people have already proved it on their own examples! So everything depends only on you and on how ready you really are to change yourself!

It is also worth noting that inside this goal we will try to get you used to proper stretching and teach you to enjoy it. Anyone who does strength training (including bodyweight training) absolutely needs regular flexibility work!

Goal #4. Change your appearance




Of course, becoming stronger and healthier is already a great goal by itself, but we should not forget that many people start training to improve how they look. We understand that perfectly, and that is why inside the program we will give you all the information you need for that!

Because no matter how hard you train, training alone (with the wrong nutrition) will not change your physique. So pay maximum attention to the infoposts of the second week of the program, which will be dedicated to nutrition.

Goal #5. Find new friends




From previous launches we can say with confidence that a large number of participants from the most different cities and countries sign up for our program. So the app gives you a unique chance to meet like-minded people from all over the world and go through the program together from the first day to the last! And knowing that someone else is going through SOTKA at the same time as you will be a great extra stimulus for productive training!

It is quite possible that you are not the only one in your city who decided to do SOTKA, and that creates a chance to organize meetings and joint sessions! We are not saying you have to gather for every workout, but even if you hold such mini-meetups once a week (for example on Sundays) that will also be an extra stimulus and a great way to spend a day off! And it is always more fun and more interesting than training alone.
